Alerts

  • EOD reports dropping: an email when fewer than 60% of the EAs who clocked in that day filed an EOD report. Checked daily. The plan says to act when it happens two working days in a row.
  • AI errors: an email when more than 2% of AI calls fail within an hour. Checked hourly.
  • AI cost spike: waits until there is a week of data to know what a normal day looks like, and is then set to warn at 50% above it.
3Fixed

Browser activity wasn't reaching PostHog

Yesterday's final setup step routed the Hub's activity through its own web address, so ad blockers can't stop it. That routing was missing PostHog's main addresses, so for about a day, activity recorded in people's browsers (pages opened, buttons pressed, AI actions) never arrived. It was found while building the AI dashboard and fixed the same afternoon; activity has been arriving normally since.

The events the database sends (tasks moved, shifts, EOD reports) and the AI cost records go to PostHog directly, so they weren't affected. The browser charts will show a gap for 22–25 October.

4Fixed

"Break down a task" asked a second question

When a task is too vague to split, the AI asks one question first, as Abder's spec describes. But after the answer it sometimes asked another question instead of giving the steps, so there was nothing to keep. Now:

  • The AI is told it may ask only once, and must give steps after an answer.
  • If it asks again anyway, the Hub asks once more, insisting on steps.
  • If that still fails, a plain message suggests adding a little more detail to the task, rather than another question.

Confirmed live: after an answer, Break down now returns its steps.

5Fixed

Closing an AI panel was counted up to three times

The Prioritize board panel can be closed three ways (the close button, Discard, or the Escape key), and a single close could be recorded more than once. That would have made the AI's suggestions look less used than they are. Each panel now counts once, and records how it was closed, as the tracking plan asks. A panel whose changes were applied is never also counted as dismissed.

6Fixed

Clicks lost when someone left the page quickly

The Hub held activity for a few seconds before sending it in a batch. Someone who clicked "Keep all" and closed the tab straight away lost that click. Activity is now sent the moment it happens. The Hub records only a handful of actions per person, so there's nothing to gain from batching, and nothing is lost when people move on quickly.
