The menu, grouped by what you're doing

"My Day" held six different kinds of page: a summary, a calendar, an inbox, a task board, a clock and a report. The menu is now split by the question you have when you reach for it:

Categories open on hover

On desktop the sidebar shows the category titles, and resting the mouse on one opens its pages in a small menu beside it, like the website menu you shared.

  • You always know where you are: the category you're in is highlighted, with the page name under it, for example "Work · Task Manager".
  • Easy to use: moving into the menu keeps it open, and brushing past another title on the way doesn't swap menus. Moving away closes it.
  • Not mouse-only: clicking a title also opens it (for touch-screen laptops and keyboards), and Esc closes it.
  • Phones have no hover, so the phone menu still lists every page and opens on tap.
If someone still sees the old menu Their browser tab was opened before the update. Pressing Ctrl+Shift+R, or closing and reopening the tab, loads the new version.
