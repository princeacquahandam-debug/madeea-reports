Abder's Academy documents, reviewed

All three documents were read in full and compared, requirement by requirement, with the Academy that went live on 16 November.

  • Sign-in and Public PRD (1 of 3): four screens. Sign in (admins land in Studio, learners in My Learning), Forgot password (same message whether or not the account exists, link valid 2 hours), Accept invitation (name and password from a link valid 14 days) and a public certificate check page, with certificate codes in the form MA-XXXX-XXXX.
  • Roadmap: five phases from November to April. Go-live and a pilot of 10 to 15 people (16 to 27 November), one sign-in with the Hub (December to mid-January), training inside the Hub's dashboard and daily plan (to mid-February), automatic enrollment and team views (to mid-March), then Arabic, phones, imports, video lessons and one academy per client (to the end of April).
  • Sign-in screens: the new look, on desktop and phone, light and dark: a striped orange brand panel beside the form, the MADE/EA ACADEMY wordmark, and its own colours and typeface.

The main finding: the documents describe Abder's own Academy app, already built, with its own database, sign-in and an AI course builder that uses Claude. They are screenshots of his code, not designs for ours.

2Decided

Decision: deploy Abder's Academy

Rather than rebuild his screens in our version, his code will be deployed as the Academy.

  • Our Academy stays live on academy.madeea.io until his is deployed and tested, so there is no gap.
  • Then the address moves to his app. The Hub's Learn menu becomes a plain link to it, until the one sign-in in his Phase 1.
  • Our Academy code is kept in the repository, unused, in case it's needed again.
  • Good news for his Phase 1: the Hub signs people in with Supabase, which is his "option B", the fastest of the three he compared.
