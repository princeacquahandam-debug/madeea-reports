A focused EA account: Clients & Files removed

As requested, EA accounts no longer have the Clients & Files section: Client Vault, Password Manager and Notes & Files are gone from their sidebar, and nothing else in their Hub points there. Managers, admins and owners keep everything as before. Both changes are live at hub.madeeas.com.

Clients & Files removed from EA accounts

Before

  • An EA's sidebar had a Clients & Files section: Client Vault, Password Manager, Notes & Files

Now

  • An EA's sidebar goes from My Day straight to Playbook
  • Managers, admins and owners still see the section

Every other way in is closed too

  • Search (Ctrl+K) no longer offers those pages to an EA.
  • The first-login guided tour skips its Clients & Files stop for EAs.
  • The client switcher at the top of the sidebar no longer shows "Open the Client Vault" to EAs.
  • Old links and bookmarks: an EA who opens one of those pages sees "isn't part of your access. Ask an admin if you need it." instead of the page.
2Live

EA Dashboard: no links to the removed pages

Two Dashboard pieces only led to Client Vault: the Clients At Risk tile and the Client Snapshot section. EAs no longer see them. Their Dashboard now shows five tiles in one even row (Tasks Active, Meetings Today, Emails Pending, Gone quiet, Automations Running), then Today's EOD, the Priority Queue and Upcoming Meetings. Managers, admins and owners see the full Dashboard as before.

What's next

  1. Gmail for the client's EA, once Google's security assessment (CASA) is arranged.
  2. Finish Google verification: the demo video and permission notes, which remove the "unverified app" warning and the 100-account limit.
  3. Microsoft publisher verification (free), so clients at larger companies can connect Outlook without asking their IT team first.
