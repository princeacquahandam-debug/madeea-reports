1Live

One sign-in from the Hub

Abder's phase one: the Academy is a separate platform, but nobody signs in twice.

  • In the Hub, Learn → MadeEA Academy opens the Academy in a new tab, already signed in. The Training page in the Hub also shows a short summary: paths assigned, lessons done, certificates.
  • On academy.madeea.io, people can also sign in with their Hub email and password, or click Continue with MadeEA Hub.
  • It is for the MadeEA team only. Client portal accounts are refused.
  • Each app keeps its own sign-in, so signing out of one never signs you out of the other by surprise.
2Live

Studio: building courses

Built by hand first, as Abder asked; AI comes next with his prompts.

  • Each course has a title, description, level, icon and cover image.
  • Modules hold lessons. A lesson can mix text, images, videos (uploaded, or a YouTube, Vimeo or Loom link) and tables, as Prince and Rowena asked.
  • Each module can have flashcards and a quiz. Quiz questions are multiple choice or free text, with a pass mark.
  • Courses can be previewed exactly as a learner sees them, published, taken back to draft or duplicated.
  • The existing Made Ready course was copied in as a draft (10 modules, 47 lessons, 64 questions), ready to edit and add to a path.
3Live

Learning paths and the final assessment

  • A learning path groups courses in order, with a type (onboarding, client ready and so on) and an optional due date.
  • Who it's for: everyone, or chosen people, regions or teams, as Abder described ("EAs in Japan, the US, the UK").
  • Final assessment (optional): pass mark, time limit, attempts allowed and how many questions each attempt asks. It opens once every lesson is done.
  • Question bank: questions are tagged easy, medium or hard. Each attempt draws a random set spread across the three, so two attempts are rarely the same. The answers never reach the learner's browser, and the exam returns only the score.
  • A path without a final assessment gives the certificate once every lesson is done.
4Live

Learners, reports, reviews and settings

  • Learners: everyone on the team, with their Academy role (learner, trainer or admin), region, team, progress, certificates and when they were last active. Select the people who aren't progressing and email them a reminder in one click. Invite new people as a learner, trainer or admin.
  • Reports: for each path, how many were assigned, started and certified, and the average exam score; progress by person; star ratings by course.
  • Reviews: learners rate each course and can leave a comment when they finish it, so the content can be improved.
  • Settings: who signs the certificate, their title, and the signature itself, uploaded as an image or drawn on screen, as Rowena asked.
5Live

The learner side

  • Overview: your learning paths with progress, "continue where you left off", and your certificates.
  • Focused player: only the content, nothing else on screen (Abder: "nothing can distract the learner"). Lessons, flashcards that flip, then the module quiz with the right answers explained.
  • Final assessment with a timer when there is one, and a pass or fail result.
  • Certificate with the learner's name, path, score, date, certificate number and the signature, ready to print or save as PDF.
