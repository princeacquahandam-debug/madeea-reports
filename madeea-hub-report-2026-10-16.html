AI quick actions on Tasks and in the Inbox

As agreed, the AI actions now sit on the page, labelled in words, and the answer opens in a pop-up over the page. Nobody has to open the chat. They pulse gently until someone has tried one, so they get noticed rather than ignored.

PageActionsWhere
TasksPrioritize board · Break down a task · Find stuck tasksBeside the title (see 1b)
InboxSummarize unread · Draft replies · Find action itemsBeside the title, as in Abder's mockup

Nothing is sent or saved without a click: drafts are there to review, and changes wait for you to apply them.

On the AI cost question from the call The buttons don't paste the whole board or inbox into the AI. Each one sends a short instruction, and Madeline then looks up only the tasks or messages it needs, a limited number at a time. That keeps each request small as an account grows. Abder can test this directly, and his definitions for each action will replace the current wording when they arrive.

Tasks actions built to Abder's answer spec

Abder's spec sets out exactly what each Tasks action reads, how it answers and what it does when there's nothing to report. All three now work that way. The answers are plain and factual, built only from the board, and nothing changes until you apply it.

ActionWhat it does now
Prioritize boardA side panel slides in from the right and leaves the board visible. It shows how many tasks were reviewed, then one line such as "2 overdue, 1 blocked. Here's the order I'd work in, with 3 suggested changes." Below that, up to 10 tasks are ranked (overdue first, then due today, then blocked or waiting on approval), each with a one-fact reason. Up to 5 get one suggested change each, such as "Assign to …", "Due date Sep 23 → Oct 15" or "Priority Normal → High", ticked by default. Apply N changes saves only what is ticked, and an Undo stays on screen for 10 seconds. It never marks a task Done, deletes or renames one, only moves due dates forward for overdue tasks, and only assigns to the client's own EA.
Break down a taskPick a card, then press the button. With no card picked it says "Pick a task first". It writes 3 to 7 short steps, each starting with a verb, in working order, with a time estimate where it can judge one. The steps appear on the card itself with Keep all / Undo. If the task is too vague, it asks one short question instead of guessing.
Find stuck tasksA card above the board, with the stuck cards outlined on the board. Stuck means overdue, blocked, no update in 7+ days, or waiting for approval 3+ days. Each row shows the task, why it's stuck, the days stuck and one action: Set new date, Nudge assignee (a comment on the task) or Open. It shows 5 rows, then "Show N more". When all is well: "Nothing is stuck. Every open task moved in the last 7 days."
Cost, the deciding factor from the call The spec's ranking and "stuck" rules are exact, so Prioritize board and Find stuck tasks are worked out directly from the board. They give the same factual answer every time, they're instant, and they use no AI at all. Only Break down a task calls the AI, on the lower-cost model, and it sends just the one task.

Questions asked in the Madeline chat stay in the chat

On the call, asking in the chat opened the answer in a pop-up. Now, anything asked or clicked inside the Madeline chat is answered in the chat. The pop-up is only for the AI action buttons on the pages.

3Live

Inbox: a channel logo on every message

Every message now carries its channel's logo (Gmail, Slack, Outlook and so on) on the corner of the sender's circle. Messages from every channel can sit in one list, and you can still see at a glance where each came from. You can see this in the Inbox screenshot above.

4Live

Learn, in plain sight

A new Learn button in the header holds "How this page works", "Replay the guided tour", the Training Center and the SOPs. Before, the guide and the tour were tucked inside the account menu. The sidebar's "Guides & Training" is now called Learn, and training videos will go in the Training Center as they are made.

5Live

Quick note can draft an email

A spoken or typed quick note can now become an email as well as a task. Draft an email sits beside "Turn into a task". Madeline works out who it's for, writes a subject line and the email, and shows the draft for you to review. Nothing is sent.

6Live

No footer, no empty space, and a Stop sharing button

  • Footer removed from every page inside the Hub, as agreed. The Privacy Policy and Terms remain on the sign-in page.
  • Empty space filled: Settings now uses two columns instead of one narrow one, and on Integrations the Google Calendar card fills its row.
  • Stop sharing: while your screen is being shared for screenshots, a "Stop sharing" button now sits beside the clock in the header. Your shift keeps running. Clocking out stops sharing too.
If someone still sees the old version Their browser tab was opened before the update. Pressing Ctrl+Shift+R, or closing and reopening the tab, loads the new version.
