1Fixed

All the AI actions on Task Manager and Calendar

  • Task Manager now offers Break into steps, Draft follow-up and Suggest priority. Before, only "Suggest priority" appeared unless a task was open. When no task is open, they apply to the most urgent task, and the follow-up goes to a task that's waiting on someone. Resting the mouse on a button shows which task it means.
  • Calendar now offers Summarize last meeting as well as "Prep me for this meeting", whenever a meeting has taken place. Before, it only appeared once a meeting had recorded notes.
2Fixed

Calendar times read am and pm

Times in the month view now read 7:10am and 5pm instead of "7:10a" and "5p", in the Hub calendar and in the client portal calendar.

3Fixed

Adding a team member

  • "Add someone" is gone. It wasn't broken: it was the already-selected half of an "Add someone / Set a password" switch, so clicking it changed nothing. You add someone with "Create account" as before. Resetting a password is now a plain link, "Already a member and can't sign in? Set a new password instead", with a way back. The same switch was also simplified on the client's People page and in Client Vault.
  • The green confirmation now sits directly above the credentials and reads "send them the credentials below".
4Fixed

The Admin page no longer flashes for EAs

While an account's role was still loading, the Admin page showed for a split second before being hidden. Restricted pages now show nothing until the role is known. For an account without access, they then say "Sorry, this page isn't available", with a Go back button and a link to the Dashboard. The same fix covers the other restricted pages, such as Client Vault and Password Manager. We tested it on a deliberately slow connection, and the Admin page never appeared for an EA account.

5–6

The other two findings

  • "Research Acme Corp before my meeting": as the review noted, the answer is expected. There's no Acme Corp data on record, so Madeline says so and lists general points to check. No change needed.
  • Google's "hasn't verified this app" warning on Integrations comes from Google, not the Hub. It goes away once the Google Cloud project owner completes Google's app verification, which is the first step under "What's next" below.
If someone still sees the old behaviour Their browser tab was opened before the update. Pressing Ctrl+Shift+R, or closing and reopening the tab, loads the new version.

What's next

  1. Finish Google verification: the demo video and permission notes, which remove the "unverified app" warning and the 100-account limit.
  2. Gmail for the client's EA, once Google's security assessment (CASA) is arranged.
  3. Microsoft publisher verification (free), so clients at larger companies can connect Outlook without asking their IT team first.
