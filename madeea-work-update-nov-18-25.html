01 · TESTING THE HUB

From "Not ready" to "Ready"

An AI tester, Grokbot, went through the live Hub seven times between 20 and 25 November. Each round, Claude found the cause of every problem, fixed it, and checked the fix before anything went live.

Testing · 20–25 Nov

How the testing worked

Each round

  1. Grokbot tests with real accounts and reports each problem with steps, a screenshot and a severity.
  2. Claude fixes: reproduces it, finds the cause, fixes it, tests the fix.
  3. The team checks the fix on a local copy, then says "push it".
  4. Claude checks it live and writes a short re-test brief covering only what changed.

Kept safe

  • The first rounds only looked at pages, never saved anything.
  • Saving, sending and inviting were tested in QA Sandbox, a separate team with its own owner, assistant and test client. It can't see any real client or record.
  • Everything the tester creates starts with "QA-", and the sandbox is cleaned after each round.
The full story, round by round, is in the QA overview.

02 · NEW IN THE HUB

What's new, and how to use it

The pieces the testing showed were missing: signing off work, answering clients, fixing what you saved, and letting clients comment on their tasks.

Work → Approvals · 23–25 Nov

Approvals: signing off work

For owners and admins

Step by step

  1. The bell shows "Waiting for approval" when someone submits work that needs sign-off.
  2. Open Work → Approvals. Three lists: Waiting for approval (work in Review first), Sent back, and Approved in the last 30 days with who approved and when.
  3. Happy with it? Click Approve.
  4. Needs changes? Click Send back, type what needs changing, and send. The task goes back to In progress with your note on it and in its comments.
  5. When the assistant resubmits, it's back under Waiting with a Resubmitted tag and your earlier note.

Good to know

  • Only owners and admins can approve. The database itself now refuses anyone else, including the person who did the work.
  • The send-back note is never shown to the client.
New in the Hub

Tasks · 23–25 Nov

Approvals: doing work that needs sign-off

For assistants

Step by step

  1. In a task's edit window, tick Needs approval before it can be completed. The card shows a Needs approval tag.
  2. When the work is ready, move it to Review. The owners and admins are told.
  3. If it's sent back, the bell shows "Sent back" with the reason, and the card has an amber Sent back tag. Make the changes, then move it to Review again; the tag becomes Resubmitted.
  4. Once it shows Approved, move it to Done.

New on 25 Nov

Trying to move an unapproved task to Done (dragging, the tick, the edit window or an AI suggestion) now says why it can't, instead of the card silently sliding back:

"…needs an owner or admin to approve it before it can be marked Done. Move it to Review and they'll see it in Approvals."
New in the Hub

Work → Client messages · 23 Nov

Reading and answering clients

Step by step

  1. The bell shows "Message from…", "Escalation from…" or "Client request".
  2. Open Work → Client messages.
  3. Assistants see each of their clients' private channels; owners, admins and managers see escalations to leadership.
  4. Pick the conversation, type the reply and click Send. The box clears straight away and shows "Sending…", so nothing goes twice.

Why it's new

Clients could already write to their assistant, or escalate to leadership, from their portal. The messages were saved, but there was no page in the Hub to read them and no alert, so they reached nobody.

Private by design: an assistant doesn't see escalations about them, and leadership doesn't see the assistant's private channel.
New in the Hub

Time & Reports · 23 Nov

The clock, Team today and timesheets

For assistants

  1. Pick the client in the sidebar, then Clock in (top bar or Time page). The first time each day it asks what the day is for.
  2. Pause for a break and Resume after; Clock out at the end. Your shift records the client, so the client sees your hours.
  3. On a bad connection, a clock action gives up after 15 seconds, says so, and lets you try again.

For owners and admins

  • Team today shows each person as On shift · client, On a break or Clocked out, with their hours, refreshed every minute.
  • The Time page shows your own day total. Use Whose timesheet to check or correct an assistant's shifts.
  • Your top-bar clock only ever shows your own shift.
New in the Hub

Hub · 19–23 Nov

Edit what you saved, and real names

Edit instead of delete-and-redo

  • Routines: Tasks → Routines, then the pencil on a routine. Routines now create their tasks (they never had on the live Hub).
  • Saved passwords: the pencil on a saved login. New passwords stay hidden until you click the eye.
  • Client notes: clients edit or delete their own notes in their portal.
  • Deleting a client or removing a client staff member now asks first.

Names, not email addresses

  • Settings → Account → Edit name changes your own name everywhere. Client logins have the same card in their portal Settings.
  • Creating a team account, a client login or client staff now asks for the person's name.
  • The starting password on People shows as dots until you click Show.
New in the Hub

Client portal · 20–23 Nov

The client portal

Comments on tasks

  1. A client opens a task in their portal and can read and add comments.
  2. On the Hub side, the agency's comments stay internal unless you tick Visible to client. Those show a "Client sees this" tag.
  3. Every comment written before 23 Nov stays internal.

Also

  • Clients see their assistant's hours and each day in Daily reports. The assistant's written end-of-day report stays private.
  • Each section has its own address (/tasks, /people…): reload stays put, Back works, and a section can be bookmarked.
  • Removing a client staff member hands their open tasks back to the account owner.
  • Buttons that were invisible (Send, the notification count) now show.

Automation · 24 Nov

End-of-day reports, emailed to the team leads

How it works

  1. An assistant submits their end-of-day report in the Hub, as usual.
  2. Within a minute, Reichelle and Rowena get an email from MadeEA Hub: the assistant's name, the date, what was done, blockers, and the plan for tomorrow, with a link to the reports.
  3. One email per assistant per day. A later correction updates the Hub without a second email.

Good to know

  • Asked for on 19 October; built in the team's automation service (n8n) and secured with a shared key.
  • Tested end to end. It stays off for the test team, so tests never email the team leads.
It only sends when reports are submitted. None had been in the last week, so the team has been reminded to file them in the Hub.

Email · 24 Nov

Hub emails in madeeas.com Spam

One-minute fix for each person

  1. In madeeas.com Gmail on a computer: gear icon → See all settings.
  2. Filters and Blocked Addresses → Create a new filter.
  3. In From, type madeea.io, then Create filter.
  4. Tick Never send it to Spam, then Create filter.
  5. Search in:spam from:madeea.io and click Report not spam on anything found.

Why it happens

Hub mail passes every sender check. But madeea.io looks very like madeeas.com, so Gmail treats it as a possible fake and may show a red "This message might be dangerous" warning.

The fix for everyone is two settings in the madeeas.com Google Admin console: approve madeea.io as a sender, and stop the lookalike warning for it. The request is with the admin.
