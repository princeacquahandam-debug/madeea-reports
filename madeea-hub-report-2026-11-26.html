What the Academy held, and the clean-up

Before presenting, the Academy's contents were checked directly in the database:

  • Made Ready (10 modules, 47 lessons, 64 quiz questions, 4 practical tasks) was there, complete, but still a draft, so no learner could see it.
  • A test course and test path from the 16 November trial were still published, so the only thing a learner could see was test material. Both were deleted.
  • The course card described itself as "brought across from the Hub's Training Center". It now has learner-facing descriptions.
  • Who writes the content: the Academy platform was built by us; Made Ready is MadeEA's own HR outline ("Made Ready Academy: AI-Powered EA Foundations"), set up as a course. New courses are built by the team in Studio, by hand first and with AI once Abder's prompts arrive.
2Fixed

Garbled characters repaired

In the lessons, bullet points showed as "â€¢" instead of "•", and dashes and arrows were garbled the same way.

  • The cause: the course files were correct. When the course was loaded into the database on 3 November, the text was copied with the wrong character setting, so every special character arrived garbled. The 16 November move into the new Academy carried it across.
  • The fix: a repair written in plain characters only, tested on a copy first, then run once. It corrected every lesson, title and quiz question in the Academy and the Hub's old Training Center, and a check confirmed none are left. Nothing outside the Academy was touched.
  • Prevented: everything copied for running in the database now uses the correct character setting.
3Live

50 flashcards added

The new Academy supports flashcards, but Made Ready had none, because the old Training Center never had them.

  • 5 cards per module, each written from that module's own lessons, with nothing new invented. The full list was reviewed and approved before anything was added.
  • Each module now has a Flashcards step before its quiz: the card shows a question, Flip shows the answer, and Next card moves on.
  • Tested on a copy first: it added 5 cards to every module, would never add duplicates or overwrite cards made by hand, and touched nothing else.
4Live

Opened to the team, in three day sessions

An EA opening the Academy saw "Nothing assigned yet". That was expected: learners only see courses through a learning path assigned to them, and Made Ready was a draft in no path. Clients cannot sign in to the Academy at all; it is for the MadeEA team only.

  • Published and placed in a new "Made Ready" learning path, open to everyone on the team. All 12 people now see it on their Overview.
  • Split into three day courses, using the day titles from HR's outline, so each day is its own session:
    • Day 1: AI-Ready Executive Assistant Foundation (modules 1–3, 17 lessons)
    • Day 2: AI Productivity Mastery for Executive Assistants (modules 4–7, 18 lessons)
    • Day 3: AI-Enhanced Executive Assistant Simulation (modules 8–10, 12 lessons)
  • Nothing was copied or deleted in the split: modules moved with their lessons, flashcards, quizzes and anyone's progress. Module numbers now start at 1 within each day.
  • A gap to know about: the four practical tasks came into the new Academy as reading pages. There is no answer box or trainer review screen yet, so learners share their work with their trainer directly.
5Done

Team sign-ins

Seven team members were to be given Academy access. The Academy uses the Hub's own accounts, and all seven already had Hub accounts, so no new accounts were needed. Their sign-ins were sorted from the Hub's Admin Panel. Anyone given a starting password should change it in the Hub under Settings → Account.

6Signed off

The Academy presentation, and the client's feedback

A 21-slide presentation of the whole Academy was published and pinned on the reports page: The MadeEA Academy: how it works, and what's ready. It covers signing in, the Made Ready course, every part of Studio step by step, the learner side, today's changes, the decisions needed, and how to change who sees Made Ready.

The client's feedback after the presentation:

  • Content complete: all 67 pages were reviewed with admin access and cross-referenced with the source document.
  • Prompt Engineering (Day 2) stood out as particularly valuable.
  • The Academy is useful internally too, as a refresher and to build on existing skills, not only for EAs. The path is already open to the whole team, leads included.

A message announcing the Academy to the team was also drafted, ready to send once the person who reviews the practical tasks is named.
