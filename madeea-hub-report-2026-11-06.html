Training Center paused

On the call, Abder said the Academy should be its own learning space (an overview, my courses, a focused lesson view, courses built by AI from uploaded material), and Prince asked for it to be hidden until that design is agreed.

  • The Training Center is gone from the sidebar, the Learn menu, Settings and the guided tour.
  • The Made Ready warnings when choosing a Lead EA are hidden too.
  • Training videos (Loom) are still under Learn → Training videos.
  • Nothing is deleted: one switch brings the course back as it was.
  • Madeline now says the Academy is paused instead of pointing people to it.

Tasks AI actions, as Abder and Rowena asked

#What was said on the callWhat changed
1"Sometimes these are disabled, I can't do any action"Prioritize no longer shows greyed-out checkboxes. Tasks with nothing to change say "No change needed"; a board with nothing to change says so and offers Done. The same board now gets the same suggestions every time.
2"The main thing is the next action … highlight it"Find stuck tasks shows each task's Next step in bold, in a highlighted line. Prioritize marks the top task as Start here.
3"Once I click outside, it should close"Prioritize, Find stuck tasks and the Break down steps close on a click anywhere else. Starting one AI action closes the others.
4A short tutorial for the shortcutsA one-time tip on the Tasks page explains the three AI buttons, and each has hover text.

Simpler task cards, due date opens the calendar

  • Cards show only the title, who it's for, the client, the due date and the priority. Notes, checklist items, files and progress live in the task's details, which keep growing.
  • Clicking a due date opens that day in the calendar, the entry point Abder suggested instead of drawing tasks on the calendar.
