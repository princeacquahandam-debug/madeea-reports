Signing in and getting around

One sign-in for the Hub and the Academy. Everything is on the left; the things you reach for all day are along the top.

Step by step

  1. Go to hub.madeea.io and sign in with the email and password your admin set. Forgot it? Use Forgot password; the reset email comes from MadeEA Hub.
  2. The left menu is grouped: Today, Work, Time & Reports, Clients & Files, Learn, Settings. Click a group to open it.
  3. Along the top: search (Ctrl K) for tasks, clients and messages, Clock in, Quick note, Learn and the bell for alerts.
  4. The orange ✨ button, bottom right, opens Madeline, the AI assistant, on any page.
  5. Your name, bottom left, opens your settings. The « next to the logo collapses the menu.

Who sees what

  • Executive assistants see their own work: tasks, time, reports.
  • Managers and admins also see the team's, and the Admin Panel.
  • Clients get their own portal, never the agency screens.
Lost on a page? Learn → “How this page works” explains the page you're on in three or four points.

01 · TODAY

Your day at a glance

Dashboard, My workspace, Calendar and Meeting Intelligence: what needs doing, who you're meeting, and what came out of those meetings.

Today

Dashboard

Left menu → Today → Dashboard

How to use it

  1. Read the greeting line: how many active tasks you have and how many need a follow-up.
  2. Check the counters: your active tasks, meetings today, emails to answer, follow-ups, automations running. Click any one to open that list.
  3. Work through Needs follow-up: anything with no update in 5+ days. Click Follow up to act, or Snooze it.
  4. Use the AI buttons at the top: Plan my day, Catch me up, Draft follow-ups.
  5. At the end of the day, the End-of-day report card shows what's filled in. Click Submit report.

What it's for

The first page of the day. Every number is live: it changes as your tasks, calendar and inbox change, so there's nothing to refresh.

Good to know: assistants see follow-ups for their own tasks only; managers see the whole team's.
Today

Today

My workspace

Left menu → Today → My workspace

How to use it

  1. Open it to see your open tasks and your EOD reports in one place.
  2. Add things you want Madeline to keep in mind, then click Remember. Your memory and personal notes are private to you.
  3. Managers can open any member's workspace to see their work and reports.

What it's for

A home for each person. Work output is visible to managers; personal memory and notes to Madeline stay private, even from managers.

Today

Calendar

Left menu → Today → Calendar · needs Google connected (Settings → Integrations)

How to add an event

  1. Open Calendar. Your Google Calendar shows next to the work it creates.
  2. Click New event.
  3. Fill in the title, date, start and end. The time zone is yours by default; guests, location and a description are optional.
  4. Click Add to Google Calendar. It appears in Google Calendar straight away, and Google emails any guests.

What it's for

Your diary and your work in one place. Click a task's due date anywhere in the Hub and the calendar opens on that day.

Times: shown in your own time zone, set in Settings. A client's local time is shown where it matters.

Today

Meeting Intelligence

Left menu → Today → Meeting Intelligence

How to use it

  1. Open it after a recorded call. It reads the Fathom transcripts.
  2. Review what it found: tasks, decisions and things to remember.
  3. Tasks it finds are already on the task board. Open them there; this page is the record of where they came from.

What it's for

Nothing agreed on a call gets lost. Action items become tasks without anyone retyping them.

02 · WORK

Inbox, tasks and routines

Where the day's work actually happens: answering mail, moving tasks, and the jobs that repeat.

Work

Inbox

Left menu → Work → Inbox · needs Gmail or Outlook connected

How to answer an email

  1. Pick a tab: All, Needs reply, Waiting on others or Done. Search and filters are on the right.
  2. Click an email. It opens on the right with the full message.
  3. Type in Reply to…, or click AI draft to have one written in the client's tone, then edit it.
  4. Click Send. It goes from your own Gmail or Outlook, in the same thread, and shows Sent.

AI shortcuts at the top

  • Summarize unread: the gist of everything new.
  • Draft replies: first drafts for what needs an answer.
  • Find action items: the asks hidden in your mail.
Private by design: your mailbox is readable only by you. Not even an admin can open it.
Work

Work

Tasks

Left menu → Work → Tasks

How to use the board

  1. Click New task (or start from Templates). Set who it's for, the client, the due date and the priority.
  2. Drag a card between columns: To Do, In Progress, Waiting, Review, Done. It saves instantly.
  3. Click a card for its notes, checklist, files and progress. Click its due date to open that day in the calendar.
  4. Hover a card for the edit and delete controls.
  5. Tasks you move to Done are added to today's EOD report automatically.

AI on the board

  • Prioritize: ranks your open tasks and marks where to start.
  • Find stuck tasks: the next action for each one that stalled.
  • Break down: writes the steps for a big task.
Tip: click anywhere outside an AI result to close it.
Work

Work

Routines (repetitive tasks)

Left menu → Work → Routines

How to set one up

  1. Click New repetitive task.
  2. Give it a title, the client, who it's for, and how often it repeats.
  3. Save. From then on the task appears on the board by itself, every time it's due.

What it's for

The weekly report, the Monday inbox clean-up, the monthly invoice run: set once, never forgotten.

03 · TIME & REPORTS

Time, screenshots and end-of-day reports

How hours are tracked for attendance and payroll, how work is evidenced, and how each day is reported.

Time & Reports

Time tracker

Clock in at the top of every page · Left menu → Time & Reports → Time tracker

A working day

  1. Click Clock in at the top when you start.
  2. Allow the browser's screen-share prompt. Screenshots and activity are only recorded while you're clocked in.
  3. Taking a break? Click Pause, then Resume. Paused time doesn't count.
  4. Click Clock out at the end of the day. The Time tracker page shows your hours as a timesheet.

What it's for

Clock-ins are used for attendance and payroll. Long days can have an automatic unpaid break deducted, set by an admin.

Nothing outside a shift is recorded. Typing is counted, never captured.
Time & Reports

Time & Reports

Screenshots

Left menu → Time & Reports → Screenshots

How to review

  1. Pick the person and the day.
  2. Each screenshot (about one every ten minutes) is shown with the activity for that period: keyboard and mouse counts and idle time.
  3. Judge each one on the question that matters: does what was happening match what was claimed?

Who can see them

The assistant sees their own; managers and admins see the team's. Kept for 90 days by default.

Read the scope note: activity counts cover the browser tab, so writing in another app shows as low activity.
Time & Reports

Time & Reports

End-of-day (EOD) reports

Left menu → Time & Reports → EOD reports, or the card on the Dashboard

Submitting yours

  1. Open My report. Completed today is already filled from the tasks you moved to Done.
  2. Add Blockers and your Plan for tomorrow.
  3. Click Submit report. Your managers are alerted.

For managers: the Team tab

  • Everyone's reports, blockers and plans in one place.
  • Completion: how many days each person submitted this month.
  • Filter by person, date, or “With blockers”, and open any report as submitted.

04 · CLIENTS & FILES

Everything about each client, kept safe

Client profiles, their logins, notes and files, and how the desk is performing for them.

Clients & Files

Client Vault

Left menu → Clients & Files → Client Vault

Adding a client

  1. Click New Client.
  2. Fill in their preferred channel, tone, tags and notes. Add a photo by uploading or pasting an image link.
  3. Choose their Lead EA. The Hub warns if that assistant hasn't finished their Made Ready training.
  4. To give the client their own portal login, open their profile and use Give access.

What it's for

One profile per client. Madeline uses each client's tone and preferences whenever she drafts for them.

Clients & Files

Clients & Files

Password Manager

Left menu → Clients & Files → Password Manager

How to use it

  1. Click Add login, then pick the client and enter the site, username and password.
  2. Admins decide which assistant can see which login.
  3. To use one, open it and copy the password. Nothing is shown to people it wasn't shared with.

What it's for

Client logins kept in one place instead of chats and spreadsheets. They're encrypted in the browser before they're stored.

Better still: ask the client for delegated access instead of a password where the service allows it.

05 · LEARN

How the work is done, written down once

SOPs, training videos and the MadeEA Academy.

Learn

SOPs (standard operating procedures)

Left menu → Learn → SOP, or the Learn menu at the top

Running a procedure

  1. Open a procedure, then pick the client and the task it's for.
  2. Tick each step as you go. Steps marked ✨ run the matching AI action and tick themselves.
  3. Pin to screen keeps the checklist floating while you work anywhere in the Hub.
  4. Finishing marks the task done, so it lands in today's EOD without retyping.

What it's for

Checklists for the work that repeats. The same procedure can run for several clients at once, each with its own progress.

Learn

Learn

Training videos

Learn menu → Training videos (the Training page)

How to add one

  1. Record the walkthrough in Loom as usual.
  2. On the Training page, click Add a Loom link and paste it.
  3. It plays right in the Hub. Write it up as an SOP so the steps outlast the video.

What it's for

Record how a job is done once, so the next person watches it instead of asking.

Recording inside the Hub switches on once a Loom developer app ID is added.

MadeEA Academy

Learn menu → MadeEA Academy · academy.madeea.io

For a learner

  1. Click Learn → MadeEA Academy. A new tab opens, already signed in.
  2. Open a learning path and press Start. The player shows only the lesson.
  3. Work through the lessons, flip the flashcards and take each module's quiz.
  4. When every lesson is done, take the final assessment and download your certificate.

For admins (Studio)

  • Build courses: lessons with text, images, video and tables, flashcards, quizzes.
  • Group them into learning paths, with a final assessment and who it's for.

06 · SETTINGS & ADMIN

Connections, automations and the team

Connecting mail and calendars, automations, managing accounts, and your own preferences.

Settings

Integrations

Left menu → Settings → Integrations

Connecting Gmail and Google Calendar

  1. On the Gmail card, click Connect, then Sign in.
  2. Google's own window opens. Choose your account; Google shows the app as MadeEA Hub.
  3. Tick all the permissions and click Continue. The window closes by itself and the card shows Connected.
  4. Click Sync now on the Google Calendar card to pull mail and events straight away.

Also available

  • Outlook and Teams with a Microsoft sign-in.
  • Slack, Discord, WhatsApp and Instagram for shared team channels.
  • Disconnect any of them at any time from the same card.
Your password never reaches MadeEA: you sign in on Google's or Microsoft's own page.
Settings

Settings

Automation

Left menu → Settings → Automation

How to use it

  1. Each automation can be set Active or Paused.
  2. Run now runs it on live data and saves the result.
  3. Open View last result to read what it produced, or build your own below.

What it's for

Recurring jobs that run for the whole workspace, so nobody has to remember them.

Settings

Settings · admins

Admin Panel

Left menu → Settings → Admin Panel (admins and owners only)

Adding a team member

  1. Under Add a team member, type their email.
  2. Choose the role: Team member for an assistant, or Manager / Admin.
  3. Copy the starting password shown, and send them the address and password yourself.
  4. Click Create account. They sign in and can change the password in Settings.

Also here

  • Every account, its role and activity.
  • Promote, demote or remove access.
  • Time-tracking rules: daily hours and the automatic unpaid break.
  • Reset a password for someone who can't sign in.
Locked down: only admins can make changes, checked on the server, and workspaces never see each other.

Madeline, the AI assistant

The orange ✨ button, bottom right of every page

How to use her

  1. Click ✨. Madeline opens on the side of whatever page you're on.
  2. Ask in plain words: “What's in my inbox?”, “Plan my day”, “Draft a reply declining Thursday”.
  3. With an email or task open, she works on that one, and offers buttons such as Summarize thread, Draft reply and Turn into task.
  4. Use the clock icon to reopen past chats. Rate an answer with 👍 / 👎.

What she knows

  • Your tasks, inbox, calendar and clients, and how the Hub works.
  • What you told her to remember in My workspace.
  • Each client's tone, when drafting for them.
Costs are capped: each person has a daily AI allowance, so usage can't run away.

07 · FOR CLIENTS

The client portal and the Delegation Coach

What a client sees when they sign in, and the coach that helps founders hand work over.

For clients

The client portal

hub.madeea.io, with the client's own login (given from Client Vault → Give access)

A client's tour

  1. Overview: where things stand today.
  2. Tasks, Daily reports and Activity: what was done, and when.
  3. Calendar and Notes: what's booked and what to know.
  4. Delegate: the Delegation Coach (next slide).
  5. Your staff: message their EA directly; there's also a line to the agency's leads.

What it's for

Clients see their own work, never the agency's screens. Every other client, the staff list and internal notes stay invisible to them.

Connected accounts: a client can share their Outlook or Google calendar with their EA from the portal.
For clients

For clients

The Delegation Coach

Client portal → Delegate · also on its own at madeea-delegation-coach.vercel.app

How a founder uses it

  1. The first time, answer “Who reports to you?”: name, title, area and MadeEA login for each person. It shows as a tree.
  2. Name up to 4 focus areas and list what's on your desk. Next week's meetings are added from your calendar.
  3. Each day the coach sorts everything into keep, your EA, a named person, or a role nobody covers yet, with a reason for each.
  4. Click Hand to [name] to put it on that person's task board in one click, or Plan it first for a full handoff message.

Choosing how much freedom

  • Check with me first: they propose, you approve.
  • Act, then tell me: they decide and keep you posted.
  • Own it outright: they handle it; you see the outcome.
Until a role exists, the coach shows the part the EA can take now, such as gathering documents or chasing.

Behind the scenes

Security and trust

What protects client data, checked ahead of Google's security assessment.

Google-approved sign-inGoogle shows “MadeEA Hub” on its consent screen, with privacy and terms pages that meet its checks.
Encrypted connectionsGmail, Calendar and Outlook access is stored encrypted only. No unencrypted copies remain.
Private mailboxesEach person's mail is readable by them alone. No administrator override.
Locked-down dataEvery table checks who's asking. Clients and other workspaces see nothing that isn't theirs.
Only MadeEA's sitesThe Hub's servers answer requests from MadeEA's own sites only, not other websites.
Verified emailHub and coach emails come from mail.madeea.io and coach.madeea.io and pass SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
