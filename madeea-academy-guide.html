The idea in one paragraph

The MadeEA Academy is where MadeEA trains its executive assistants. It is a separate site from the Hub, so learning gets its own focused space, but it uses the same accounts, so nobody signs in twice. Admins and trainers build courses in the Studio, group them into learning paths for the right people, and follow progress. EAs work through the courses in a distraction-free player, take quizzes and a final assessment, and earn a signed certificate. AI course creation comes next, once Abder hands over the prompts.

Learning paths: who learns what

A course on its own is just content. A learning path (Abder also called it a program) is how a course reaches people: it sits on top of one or more courses and decides who takes them and how they are assessed.

PartWhat it doesWhat it's forStatus
Courses in a pathPick one or more published courses and put them in order. Give the path a type (onboarding, client ready and so on) and an optional due date.Turn single courses into a complete programme.Live
AudienceEveryone, or chosen people, regions or teams.Abder's example: give a course only to EAs in Japan, the US or the UK.Live
Final assessmentOptional exam at the end, with a pass mark, a time limit and a number of attempts allowed. It opens once every lesson is done.Prove the EA has learned it, not just clicked through.Live
Question bankA pool of exam questions tagged easy, medium or hard. Each attempt draws a random set across the three levels. Learners only ever see their score, never the answers.Abder's design: a large bank (200–300 questions), 20–30 drawn per attempt, so attempts differ and can't be memorised.Live
Generate the bank with AIWill create the bank of questions from the path's course content, split by difficulty.Saves writing hundreds of questions by hand.Needs Abder's prompts
