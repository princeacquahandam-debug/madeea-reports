How to use the Delegation Coach

madeea-delegation-coach.vercel.app

Step by step

  1. Sign up or Sign in. Forgot the password? Forgot password? sends a reset link from no-reply@coach.madeea.io.
  2. Take the delegation assessment (it can be retaken any time).
  3. Start a plan: describe the task, then Get AI Clarification for a sharper outcome, context, success criteria and questions.
  4. Build the plan: success criteria, risks, a check-in schedule, coaching tips and a ready-to-send handoff message.
  5. Follow up from the dashboard: upcoming check-ins, Mark done with notes, and My Plans with a status for each.

Also

  • Export a plan as a PDF or add check-ins to your calendar.
  • Drafts show Finish setup until they're complete.

02 · THE COACH FOR FOUNDERS

Rebuilt from the 11 November call

In the client portal's Delegate tab: the founder keeps what only they should do, and everything else goes to the right person in one click. Live 12 November.

Client portal → Delegate · 12 Nov

Step 1: the founder's organisation

How the founder sets it up

  1. Sign in to the client portal on hub.madeea.io and open Delegate.
  2. The first time, it asks "Before you start: who reports to you?"
  3. For each person: name, title, area (finance, product, commercial…), what they handle, and their MadeEA login if they have one.
  4. It shows as a tree: the founder, then the EA, then the direct reports. Edit it any time, or skip for now.

Why first

As Rowena asked: the coach can only route work to the right person once it knows who the people are. This is Abder's tree from the call.

Coach for founders

Client portal → Delegate · 12 Nov

Step 2: keep or delegate, sorted every day

How it works

  1. Name up to 4 focus areas, and keep a list of what's on your desk.
  2. The next 7 days of meetings are added from your connected calendar.
  3. Once a day, when you open Delegate, Madeline sorts every item into keep, the EA, a named person, or a role nobody covers yet, with a one-line reason.
  4. Read the count line, e.g. "Keep 2 of 8; 5 to the EA, 1 to the Head of Product".

The rules it follows

  • Follow-ups, emails, scheduling and chasing go to the EA.
  • Department work goes to whoever covers it.
  • Meetings that name the founder, and one-to-ones, stay with the founder. Trainings are never handed on.
Coach for founders

Client portal → Delegate · 12 Nov

Step 3: hand it over, to the right person

Step by step

  1. On any item going to someone else, click Hand to [name]. It lands on that person's task board, with the coach's reason in the notes.
  2. The item then shows Handed to [name] and leaves the founder's desk list.
  3. For someone without a MadeEA login, click Plan it first: a full delegation plan with a handoff message to copy and send.
  4. For a role the business doesn't have yet, read "Until then, [EA] can: …" and hand that part to the EA in one click.

Choose how much freedom

  • Check with me first: they propose, you approve.
  • Act, then tell me: they decide and keep you posted.
  • Own it outright: they handle it; you see the outcome.
Also fixed (12 Nov): clients' AI had been blocked since 7 Nov by the new cost limits. Clients now get the same daily allowance as the team.

Connecting Gmail and Calendar

  1. In the Hub: Settings → Integrations, then Gmail → Connect → Sign in.
  2. Google's own window opens and names the app MadeEA Hub. Choose your account.

04 · MADEEA ACADEMY

academy.madeea.io

Built from Abder's roadmap on the 16 November call and live the same day, with one sign-in from the Hub. Abder's own build will replace it.

Academy · 16 Nov

Opening the Academy

Step by step

  1. In the Hub, click Learn → MadeEA Academy. A new tab opens, already signed in.
  2. Or go to academy.madeea.io and click Continue with MadeEA Hub, or use your Hub email and password.
  3. The Hub's Training page shows a summary: paths assigned, lessons done, certificates.
  4. Admins and trainers switch between Learn and Studio at the top.

Who can get in

The MadeEA team only. Client portal accounts are refused.

Academy

Academy → Studio · 16 Nov

Building a course

Step by step

  1. Studio → Courses → New course. It starts with Module 1 and Lesson 1 ready.
  2. Set the title, description, level, icon and cover image.
  3. Write each lesson: add Text, Image, Video (upload or a YouTube, Vimeo or Loom link) and Table blocks in any order.
  4. Add flashcards and a quiz (multiple choice or free text) to each module, with a pass mark.
  5. Preview it as a learner sees it, then Save and publish.

Already in it

The Made Ready course was copied across as a draft: 10 modules, 47 lessons, 64 questions, ready to review and publish.

Academy

Academy → Studio · 16 Nov

Learning paths and the final assessment

Step by step

  1. Studio → Learning paths → New learning path: title, type, optional due date.
  2. + Add a course… and put the courses in order.
  3. Tick Final assessment: pass mark, questions per attempt, time limit, attempts allowed.
  4. Fill the question bank, each question tagged easy, medium or hard. Every attempt draws a random set across the three.
  5. Who it's for: everyone, or chosen people, regions or teams. Then Save and publish.

Fair by design

Answers never reach the learner's browser; the assessment returns only the score, so the bank can't be collected one attempt at a time.

Academy

Academy → Learn · 16 Nov

Taking a path, to the certificate

Step by step

  1. On Overview, open your learning path and click Start.
  2. The player shows only the lesson. Click Next through lessons, flip the flashcards, take the quiz (right answers explained, retry any time).
  3. At the end, rate the course and leave a comment.
  4. When every lesson is done, Start the final assessment, answer, Submit.
  5. Pass, and click View your certificate: name, path, score, date, number and signature, to print or save as PDF.

For admins

  • Learners: roles, progress, email reminders, invites.
  • Reports and Reviews: who finished, scores, ratings.
  • Settings: who signs the certificate, with an uploaded or drawn signature.

05 · HUB FIXES, EMAIL AND SECURITY

Safer and tidier, 19 November

Three fixes found while setting up the reviewer account, MadeEA's own email sending, and a security pre-check ahead of Google's assessment.

Hub · 19 Nov

Three fixes

Change your own name

  1. Click your name, bottom left, or open Settings.
  2. In Account, click Edit name.
  3. Type it and click Save. The greeting, initials, team list and your tasks update.

Also fixed

  • Follow-ups show your own work. A new assistant was shown other people's stale tasks; now assistants see their own, managers the team's.
  • No false warning after connecting Google. "The login window was closed before it finished" appeared even when connecting worked. Now the window closes cleanly.
Hub fixes

Hub · 19 Nov

The Hub sends its own emails

Resetting a Hub password

  1. On hub.madeea.io, click Forgot password and enter your email.
  2. An email arrives within seconds from MadeEA Hub <no-reply@mail.madeea.io>.
  3. Click the link and choose a new password.

What changed

  • A verified MadeEA sending address instead of a shared sender limited to a few emails an hour; now up to 100 an hour.
  • Passes SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
  • Abder's Academy can send its invitations from it too.
