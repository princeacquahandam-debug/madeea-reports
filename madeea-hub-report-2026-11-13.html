The old Hub address works again

hub.madeeas.com, the address the team used before, was showing a "not found" page. It now forwards to hub.madeea.io, keeping the page someone asked for.

  • Old bookmarks and links in past emails still work. For example, hub.madeeas.com/inbox opens hub.madeea.io/inbox.
  • Checked live: the Inbox, the Privacy Policy and the Terms all open on the new address.
  • The spare madeea.io entry in Vercel was checked: it had already been removed, so nothing was left to clean up.

4When Google asks

Google's security assessment, priced

Because the Hub reads Gmail, Google requires a yearly security assessment (CASA) by one of its approved labs once the Gmail review starts. Google tells us when it is needed. We looked at all 10 approved labs.

5Final check

Delegation Coach emails from coach.madeea.io

The Delegation Coach's sign-in emails, such as password resets, now go out from no-reply@coach.madeea.io through Resend, a sending service, instead of the basic built-in sender, which only allows a few emails an hour.

  • Why coach.madeea.io: madeea.io itself is already claimed in Resend by another account. Using its own sub-address also leaves the company mailboxes and the hello.madeea.io marketing emails untouched.
  • Done: 3 records added to madeea.io's settings at Hostinger, all confirmed by Resend. The coach's sign-in emails are set to send through Resend.
  • Waiting: Resend's final approval of the address. A password reset tried before that failed, as expected. Once Resend shows "Verified", one more reset test confirms it.
