1Ready

An account for Google's reviewers

Google's Gmail review needs a demo video and an account its reviewers can sign in with. That account now exists and works end to end.

  • A new Gmail account, added to the Hub as an executive assistant named "MadeEA Review".
  • Five ordinary work emails sent to it: a meeting move, a report request, an invoice, a scheduling question and a lunch invitation.
  • Gmail and Google Calendar connected through the Hub's sign-in, showing the "MadeEA Hub" name Google approved on 12 November.
  • Checked live: the emails appear in the Hub's Inbox, a reply sent from the Hub arrived threaded in the sender's Gmail, and an event added in the Hub appeared in Google Calendar.

What's left before submitting to Google is recording the demo video with this account.

2Live

Three fixes found while setting it up

  • Follow-ups show only your own work. A brand-new assistant with no tasks was shown five of other people's tasks under "Needs follow-up". Assistants now see follow-ups for their own tasks only; managers and admins still see the whole team's.
  • No false warning after connecting Google. Connecting Gmail or Calendar worked, but the Hub then said "The login window was closed before it finished". Google's sign-in pages cut the link back to the Hub's window, so the result now reaches the Hub another way, and the sign-in window closes by itself. This affected everyone who connected Google.
  • Change your own name. Settings → Account now has Edit name. Until now a name came from the email address and could only be fixed by hand in the database. It updates the greeting, initials, the team list and tasks assigned to you.
3Live

The Hub sends its own emails

Password resets and sign-in emails from the Hub now come from MadeEA Hub <no-reply@mail.madeea.io>, through the same sending service as the Delegation Coach, instead of a shared built-in sender that allows only a few emails an hour.

  • A new sending address, mail.madeea.io, verified within 10 minutes. The website, company email, n8n and marketing email settings were not touched.
  • The Hub's limit raised to 100 emails an hour.
  • Tested: a password reset arrived in 1 second and passed all three email authentication checks (SPF, DKIM and DMARC).
  • Abder's Academy can send its invitations and password resets from the same address.
4Fixed

Security pre-check for Google's assessment

Once the Gmail review starts, Google requires a paid security assessment (CASA). The Hub was checked against it first, so it can pass on the first attempt instead of paying for extra rounds.

What was checkedResult
Who can read the team's name listsTwo small lists of team names, with some email addresses, could be read from outside the Hub without signing in. Closed, and checked from outside: access is now refused.
How Gmail, Calendar and Outlook tokens are storedTokens were encrypted, but an older second copy of 6 Google tokens was kept unencrypted. Eleven server functions were moved to the encrypted copy, the unencrypted ones were deleted, and a database rule now refuses any new one. 0 left, and nobody had to reconnect. Mail sync, sending and calendar events were retested live.
Libraries the Hub is built on7 known issues, 3 high risk, in the Loom recorder's components and the page-routing library. Both updated. 0 left; every page checked after the update.
Web security settingsSecure connection forced, protection against framing and content sniffing, strict content rules. Already in place.
Secrets in the Hub's codeNone found in what the browser downloads. Already fine.
