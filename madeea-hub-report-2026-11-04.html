Finish-by dates and approval for client work

Yesterday's Academy showed who was Made Ready, but nobody could set a deadline, and passing the course was the only check before client work. Two steps are now added, both under Training Center → Team.

A date to finish by

  • An admin picks a Finish by date for each person.
  • The learner sees "Due 12 Nov" on their course page. Once the date passes without the certificate, the badge turns to Overdue for them and for the admin.

Approved for client work

  • Once someone is Made Ready, an admin clicks Approve for client work. The approval can be taken back.
  • Nobody can approve themselves, and nobody can be approved before they're Made Ready. The database enforces both, so they can't be bypassed.
  • The certificate now says who approved them and when ("Approved for client work by … on 4 Nov 2026"). Until then it reads "Waiting for an admin to approve you for client work."
  • In the Client Vault, the Lead EA list marks anyone who is Made Ready but not yet approved, and shows a warning. Like yesterday's warning, it doesn't block saving.

The Hub for every role on the team

The 30 October message said the EOS now supports more than Executive Assistants: AI Specialists, Marketing Specialists and other roles too. The Hub treated everyone who wasn't an admin as an "EA". It now keeps two things apart:

Access level

  • What someone can see and change: Owner, Admin, Manager, Team member.
  • Unchanged, except "EA" now reads "Team member".

Job (new)

  • What they do: Executive Assistant, AI Specialist, Marketing Specialist, Operations, or Support.
  • Gives no extra access. Set per person in the Admin Panel.

Where the job shows

  • Admin Panel: a job picker next to each person. The "EAs" count now counts people whose job is Executive Assistant.
  • Sidebar, account menu, My workspace and Team Workload show the job under each name, for example "Marketing Specialist", or "Admin · Operations" for leaders.
  • Training Center: people who aren't EAs see a note that the Made Ready Academy is the Executive Assistant track, that training for their role will be added, and that they're welcome to take it.
  • Madeline, the AI assistant, knows each person's job and now helps with marketing, social media content ideas, recruitment posts, AI tools, prompts and automations, as well as EA work. Before, she turned these away as off-topic. The writing tools use the same wider scope.

Everyone was given a starting job when the update ran: Owners, Admins and Managers became Operations, and everyone else Executive Assistant. Each person's job was then set by hand in the live Admin Panel.

Brand wording and talent regions

The same message asked to stop highlighting Filipino EAs and not to name talent regions yet. The Hub, its AI prompts and the lead follow-ups were checked: none of them say "Filipino" or name a region, so nothing needed rewording.

For the countries the team will hire from later, the time zone list now always includes Bogotá, Guatemala and Nairobi, alongside Manila. Older browsers that can't list every time zone get them too, so EAs there can pick their own.
