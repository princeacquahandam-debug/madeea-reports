Madeline AI behaviour & UX

Direct data synthesis

Madeline no longer tells the EA to go and check their email. For a meeting prep she reads it all herself, in one go:

  • recent emails from every attendee, including the message text;
  • notes from past meetings with the same people;
  • related tasks, the client's profile, and a matching SOP if the meeting has a clear topic.

The brief comes back under the same short headings every time: Who's attending · Last time · Still open · Recent mail · Bring or decide. If something can't be found, she says in one line what she looked for. "Plan my day" and "What needs attention?" now name the actual meetings, tasks and emails, most urgent first.

Instant one-click actions

"Prep me" on the Dashboard, the Calendar and the meeting details used to put text in Madeline's chat box and wait for the EA to press Send. Every one-click action now runs immediately.

Results in a pop-up on the page

The answer opens in a clean pop-up over the page the EA is on, instead of the sidebar chat. It keeps everything an answer has: the sources it used, next-step buttons, the "Create task" and "Add to calendar" cards, Copy, and 👍/👎. Continue in Madeline opens the sidebar on the same answer for follow-up questions.

One thing that improves over time "Last time" fills in from recorded meetings. As Meeting Intelligence syncs your Fathom calls, briefs will start including past decisions automatically.

Task management & workflows

The task board now has a Follow-up column, for work waiting on someone else: a client's reply, a file, a sign-off.

To DoIn ProgressFollow-upReviewDone
  • Tasks in Follow-up still count as open work everywhere: the EOD plan, Madeline's answers, and the Gone quiet list.
  • Clients see them as "In progress" in their portal.

Two fixes that went out with it

  • SOP "Create one for me" was assigning the new task to a test account, so it most likely failed to save. It now goes to the person running the SOP.
  • Owners can now approve work waiting for sign-off. Before, only admins saw the Approve button.

The productEA Hub, Command Center, MadeEA OSMadeEA Hub

  • Task history read "moved it from todo to in_progress"; it now reads "To Do" and "In Progress".
