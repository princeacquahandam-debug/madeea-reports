01 · GETTING IN

One sign-in for the Hub and the Academy

The Academy is a separate, focused site, but it uses the same accounts as the Hub.

Getting in

How to open the Academy

Step by step

  1. From the Hub: click Learn → MadeEA Academy. A new tab opens, already signed in.
  2. Or go straight to academy.madeea.io and click Continue with MadeEA Hub.
  3. Or sign in there with your usual Hub email and password.
  4. Admins and trainers switch between Learn and Studio at the top.

Who gets in, and as what

  • The MadeEA team only. Client portal accounts are refused.
  • Hub owners and admins are Academy admins. Everyone else is a learner.
  • To make someone a trainer or admin in the Academy only: Studio → Learners. It does not change their role in the Hub.
Passwords are the Hub's: change yours in the Hub under Settings → Account.

Studio

Studio → Courses

Building a course

Step by step

  1. Courses → New course. It starts with Module 1 and Lesson 1 ready.
  2. Set the title, description, level, icon and a cover image.
  3. Write each lesson from blocks, in any order: Text, Image, Video (upload, or a YouTube, Vimeo or Loom link) and Table.
  4. Add flashcards and a quiz to each module: multiple choice, or free text with a model answer, plus an explanation.
  5. Preview it as a learner sees it.
  6. Save keeps it a draft; Save and publish makes it available to paths.

Good to know

  • Modules and lessons reorder with the arrows; the bin removes them.
  • Duplicate copies a course to start a variation.
  • "Create with AI" is shown as soon: it waits on Abder's prompts.
Manual first, AI second, as Abder asked on the 16 Nov call.
Studio

Studio → Learning paths

Learning paths: who learns what

Step by step

  1. Learning paths → New learning path: title, type (for example Onboarding) and an optional due date.
  2. + Add a course… and put the courses in order. Only published courses can be added.
  3. Tick Final assessment: pass mark, questions per attempt, time limit and attempts allowed.
  4. Fill the question bank, each question tagged easy, medium or hard.
  5. Who it's for: everyone, or chosen people, regions or teams.
  6. Save and publish. To pause a path later, Take offline.

Why paths matter

Learners only see courses through a path assigned to them. A course on its own is just content; the path decides who takes it and how it is assessed.

Fair by design: each exam attempt draws a random set across the three levels, and learners only ever see their score, never the answers.

Studio → Learners · Reports · Reviews

Following the team's progress

Learners

  • Everyone on the team, with their Academy role (learner, trainer or admin), region and team.
  • Progress, certificates and last activity for each person.
  • Filter those not progressing and email them a reminder.
  • Set region and team here, so paths can target them.

Reports and Reviews

  • Reports: for each path, how many are assigned, started and certified, and the average exam score; progress by person; ratings by course.
  • Reviews: the star rating and comment each learner leaves at the end of a course.
Studio

Studio → Settings

Setting up the certificate

Step by step

  1. Open Studio → Settings.
  2. Set the Academy name shown on certificates.
  3. Enter who signs the certificate and their title.
  4. Add the signature: upload an image, or draw it on screen.
  5. Save.

On every certificate

The learner's name, the path, their score, the date, a certificate number and the signature, as Rowena asked. Learners can print it or save it as a PDF.

04 · THE LEARNER SIDE, FOR EAS

Learn, practise, pass, get certified

A distraction-free player: "only the content needs to be there, nothing else", as Abder put it.

Learn

Taking a learning path

Step by step

  1. On Overview, open the learning path and click Start, or Continue where you left off.
  2. Read each lesson and click Next. The ≡ outline jumps to any module.
  3. Flip the flashcards, then take the quiz: marked straight away, with the right answer explained. Retry any time.
  4. At the end of a course, rate it and leave a comment.
  5. When every lesson is done, Start the final assessment, answer and Submit.
  6. Pass, then View your certificate.

Good to know

  • Progress is saved as you go.
  • The final assessment can have a timer and a limited number of attempts.
  • Certificates earned stay on the Overview.
